Point of View

The spreadsheet isn't the strategy

Too many organizations deploy efficiency models and processes designed for manufacturing, supply chains, and even corporate finance in an attempt to improve health care delivery. The results are predictable, and predictably bad. Data and AI can inform a decision — they can't replace the judgment of the teams doing the work, or the people those teams serve.

The plans that hold up are the ones built from the perspective of the people delivering care every day. Understand that, and the strategy follows.
